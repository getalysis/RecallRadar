What Is RecallRadar?

RecallRadar pulls official recall data directly from the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC), the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), and the FDA's openFDA database, and combines them into a single searchable feed. Instead of checking four separate government websites, you can search or subscribe to one place. Data refreshes daily via an automated pipeline pulling straight from each agency's official API.

Why Recalls Matter

Thousands of products, vehicles, foods, and medications are recalled every year for safety reasons ranging from choking hazards to fire risk to contamination. Most people never hear about a recall unless they happen to catch a news segment — RecallRadar exists so you can search by keyword or brand any time, or subscribe to a category's RSS feed to get notified automatically.

FAQ

Where does this data come from?

Directly from official government sources: CPSC's SaferProducts.gov API, NHTSA's recall API, and the FDA's openFDA API. RecallRadar does not editorialize or add unverified information — it aggregates and re-presents official data.

How often is it updated?

Once per day via an automated job that pulls fresh data from each government source.

How do I get alerts for a specific category?

Subscribe to any of the RSS feeds linked above in a free reader like Feedly or Inoreader, or connect them to a free IFTTT/Zapier recipe to get an email or push notification whenever a new item is added.
