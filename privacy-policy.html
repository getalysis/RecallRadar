Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

What We Collect

RecallRadar's search and vehicle lookup tools run entirely in your browser. We do not require an account, and we do not collect or store your search queries, vehicle information, or any personal data on our own servers.

Third-Party Services

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns. These services use cookies and similar technologies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure site usage. Google may collect and process data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of interest-based advertising industry-wide at aboutads.info.

Data Sources

Recall data displayed on this site is sourced directly from public APIs operated by the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC), the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), and the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA). We do not modify the substance of this data. Your use of the vehicle lookup tool sends your search terms (make, model, year) directly to NHTSA's public API from your browser; RecallRadar does not log or store these queries.

RSS Feeds

Our RSS feeds are static files with no tracking. If you subscribe via a third-party reader (e.g. Feedly, Inoreader) or automation service (e.g. IFTTT), that service's own privacy policy governs how they handle your subscription.
