About RecallRadar

RecallRadar is a free, independent tool that aggregates official recall data published by three US government agencies — the Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC), the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), and the Food and Drug Administration (FDA, via openFDA) — into a single searchable feed.

Before RecallRadar, checking whether a product, vehicle, food item, or medication you own has been recalled meant visiting several different government websites, each with its own search interface. RecallRadar pulls fresh data from each agency's official public API once a day and presents it in one place, searchable by keyword or brand, with free RSS feeds per category so you can be notified automatically.

RecallRadar is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any government agency. All recall data is sourced directly and unmodified from official public APIs; always confirm critical safety information at the official agency source linked on every listing.

No account, sign-up, or personal information is required to use RecallRadar's search or RSS feeds.
